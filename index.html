Nio prompter, nio sorters bilder

Ladda upp ett foto, klistra in en prompt, och få tillbaka något helt annat: ett Panini-fotbollskort, en 3D-figur, ett proffsporträtt, en kändisselfie och mer. Nio färdiga prompter, kopiera och kör.

Gemini eller ChatGPT 9 prompter Ladda upp en bild

Så gör du

Samma tre steg för alla nio: ladda upp, klistra in, byt ut hakparenteserna.

Klistra in prompterna i en AI-bildmodell som kan redigera en uppladdad bild. Jag använder Gemini (Nano Banana) eller ChatGPT. Prompterna är på engelska, men resultatet blir vad du vill ändå. Vissa prompter har inget att fylla i, då klistrar du bara in. De med hakparentes byter du ut mot dina egna uppgifter.

Resultatet varierar mellan verktyg och försök. Blir det fel, säg det i klartext eller kör om. Vissa verktyg nekar kända ansikten (som i kändisselfien), testa ett annat om det blir stopp.

Fotbollskort (VM)

Porträtt blir ett Panini-spelarkort i VM-stil.

Ladda upp 1 porträttFyll i 5 fält
Byt ut det här
  • [FIRST NAME LAST NAME] ditt namn
  • [DATE] födelsedatum
  • [HEIGHT] längd, t.ex. 182 cm
  • [WEIGHT] vikt, t.ex. 78 kg
  • [CLUB / COUNTRY] och [COUNTRY] klubb eller land, och landet vars flagga du vill ha
Prompt · klistra in med din bild
Transform the uploaded portrait into a premium football trading card inspired by World Cup sticker albums. The person is a football player named [FIRST NAME LAST NAME], born [DATE], height [HEIGHT], weight [WEIGHT], playing for [CLUB / COUNTRY]. Use the flag of [COUNTRY]. Keep the face recognizable and realistic. Create a retro 2026 World Cup card design with background, bold "26" graphic, trophy icon, vertical side design, dark rounded nameplates, white uppercase typography, and nostalgic Panini-style football sticker aesthetics
02

3D-figur

Porträtt blir en glansig 3D-figur i Pixar-stil.

Ladda upp 1 porträttInget att fylla i
Prompt · klistra in med din bild
Transform the person from the attached photo into a polished, stylized 3D cartoon chibi/figure that matches the reference style. Keep the face fully recognizable, but exaggerate proportions with a slightly elongated head, large expressive eyelids, and soft rounded forms. Use a glossy plastic-like skin surface with smooth gradients and subtle specular highlights. Preserve the original hair style and accessories in a simplified, stylized way. Light the character with soft studio lighting and a gentle rim light for separation, set against a solid color background. The final result should feel clean, high-end, and Pixar-inspired, with the look of a professional 3D render and minimal surface noise.

4K-uppskalning

Suddigt foto blir skarpt magasinsporträtt.

Ladda upp 1 fotoFyll i 4 val
Byt ut det här
  • [4K/8K] upplösning
  • [warm/cool/neutral] färgton
  • [50/75/100] skärpa i procent
  • [subtle/medium/strong] styrka på HDR
Prompt · klistra in med din bild
Take the uploaded photo and remaster it to [4K/8K] resolution. Enhance skin texture with natural detail, sharpen eyes and hair strands, boost [warm/cool/neutral] tones, increase clarity to [50/75/100]%, add [subtle/medium/strong] HDR effect. Keep the original composition. Output: ultra-realistic, magazine-quality portrait.

Proffsporträtt

Vardagsfoto blir dramatiskt studioporträtt på orange/röd bakgrund.

Ladda upp 1 ansiktsfotoInget att fylla i
Prompt · klistra in med din bild
Reference Face :
Use the attached image for face only. Preserve exact facial identity, skin tone, texture, and hairstyle. Do not modify, beautify, or change proportions. Ignore pose and clothing from reference. Pose & Direction
Low-angle dominant pose. Body slightly left, face looking right side of frame. Gaze slightly upward. Do NOT flip or mirror.Styling & Lighting
Fitted black polo shirt. Dramatic high-contrast lighting with strong left key light and soft rim light. Orange to deep red smooth gradient background.
Technical Settings
Aspect ratio 2:3 vertical (Pinterest). 4K quality. 85mm portrait look, shallow depth of field, sharp eyes, natural skin texture, no text, no watermark.

Jätteballonger

Behåller rummet exakt och trycker in gigantiska folieballonger.

Ladda upp 1 foto på en platsFyll i texten
Byt ut det här
  • [LOVE YOU] texten eller siffran du vill ha som ballonger
Prompt · klistra in med din bild
Using the attached image as an exact base, preserve the entire original location without any changes: no changes to the architecture, layout, lighting, colors, perspective, or existing objects. The photograph must remain completely untouched and recognizable.
Add only a giant, almost hyper-realistic inflatable inscription: [LOVE YOU].
Each letter or number must be a separate oversized inflatable object, individually placed and scaled, far larger than human height. All symbols are made of bright yellow glossy foil, with thick inflatable material, visible seams, soft folds, and realistic deformation caused by air pressure.
The individual inflatable balloons aggressively fill and overcrowd the space, pressing against the walls, ceiling, floor, and furniture. Each symbol bends, compresses, and deforms naturally where it touches other surfaces, with realistic weight, distance, depth, and correct perspective.

Byt outfit

Sätter plagget från bild 2 på personen i bild 1, allt annat orört.

Ladda upp 2 bilder (person + plagg)Fyll i 2 val
Byt ut det här
  • [left/right/above] varifrån ljuset kommer
  • [fashion editorial / lookbook / street style / studio campaign] stil
Prompt · klistra in med dina bilder
Photo 1 is the reference model. Photo 2 is the outfit/garment to dress them in. Realistically place the exact outfit from Photo 2 onto the model in Photo 1. Match: fabric drape and weight to the model's body position, lighting and shadow from the original scene (light source: [left/right/above]), fabric texture and color exactly as shown in Photo 2. Keep: model's face, hair, skin tone, pose, and background completely unchanged. Fit the garment naturally to the model's proportions. Result must look like the original photo, not a composite. Style: [fashion editorial / lookbook / street style / studio campaign].

Kändisselfie

Din selfie blir en bild där du står bredvid en kändis.

Ladda upp 1 porträtt av digFyll i 4 fält
Byt ut det här
  • [celebrity name] kändisen
  • [red carpet / backstage / casual street / restaurant] plats
  • [flash selfie / natural / studio] ljus
  • [slight motion blur / bokeh background / crowd in background] bakgrundseffekt
Prompt · klistra in med din bild
Using the uploaded photo as the base person, create a realistic POV selfie with [celebrity name] standing next to them. Location: [red carpet / backstage / casual street / restaurant]. Lighting: [flash selfie / natural / studio]. Both people looking at the camera, natural expressions. Add [slight motion blur / bokeh background / crowd in background]. Style: candid celebrity encounter photo.
